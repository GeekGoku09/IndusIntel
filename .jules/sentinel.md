## 2025-05-22 - Dual-Path XML Generation and Escaping Parity
**Vulnerability:** The backend endpoint (`/api/syndicate-export`) sanitized product attributes with `escapeXml` before generating BMEcat 2005 XML, but the client-side component (`SyndicationExporter.tsx`) constructed XML strings via raw template string interpolation without escaping. Product names, descriptions, or technical specifications containing characters such as `<`, `>`, `&`, `'`, or `"` led to XML injection and corrupted payloads when syndicated to downstream ERP/procurement systems.
**Learning:** When client-side components mirror server-side format serializers (e.g. for offline preview, direct clipboard copying, or client-triggered downloads), sanitization routines must be kept in parity across both boundaries.
**Prevention:** Always sanitize and XML-encode user- and model-generated attribute inputs before embedding them into XML templates on both client and server, or share a single validated serializer module.
