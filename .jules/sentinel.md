## 2026-03-31 - Neutralizing CSV Formula Injection in Industrial Syndication Exports
**Vulnerability:** CSV syndication exports (SAP Ariba / PIM CSV) both in `server.ts` and `src/components/SyndicationExporter.tsx` wrapped product fields in double quotes without checking for leading formula triggers (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, `\t`, `\r`), leaving downstream procurement spreadsheet software vulnerable to formula injection / CWE-1236.
**Learning:** Industrial component data ingested from OCR nameplates, untrusted datasheets, or model codes frequently contain leading characters like `-` or `+` (e.g. temperature ranges, +/- tolerances, or model prefixes). If an adversarial or malformed datasheet injects `=CMD|...` or `@SUM...`, opening exported Ariba CSVs in Excel/Calc could execute arbitrary commands or leak data.
**Prevention:** Always sanitize spreadsheet export fields by checking for `/^[=+\-@\t\r]/` and prepending a single quote `'` in addition to escaping double quotes (`""`).
